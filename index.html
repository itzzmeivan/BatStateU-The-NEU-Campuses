Batangas State University The National Engineering University

Batangas State University (BatStateU - The NEU) is a premier educational institution in the Philippines, known for its commitment to academic excellence and research. Established in 1903, BatStateU has grown to become one of the leading universities in the country, offering a diverse range of programs and courses across various fields of study.
